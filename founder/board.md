# Verse — founder board, 2026-11-06

Advisory result: **3 FUND IF; average 5.3/10** (Offers 5, Monopoly 4, Product 7). Three separate subagents applied the installed founder-board lenses and wrote the linked memos. These are qualitative judgements using published frameworks, not endorsements, simulated customers, financial projections or demand validation.

## Shared risks, in priority order

1. Trust in the creative catalogue: recording, recovery, backup and device transitions must preserve irreplaceable work. The current test evidence is insufficient for a commercial reliability promise.
2. One founder's clear need does not establish a reachable paying segment. Observe other writers using their actual ideas and returning later; test payment interest separately.
3. Scope and operating capacity: supporting web/iOS/Android, sync, audio and customer operations can consume the effort needed to make capture excellent. Agree rollout, budget and support ownership.

## Where the lenses disagree

- [Product](board/product.md) sees the strongest evidence: a coherent capture-and-return experience, plus a UI the founder already likes. Its 7/10 supports focused product development conditioned on reliability.
- [Offers](board/offers.md) scores 5/10 because likelihood of success, buyer reach, payment interest and service economics remain open.
- [Monopoly](board/monopoly.md) scores 4/10 because the feature bundle establishes neither a durable advantage nor a distribution route. It asks for a narrow initial segment and evidence that the workflow is materially better than its alternatives.

## Strongest version

Verse is a trusted songwriting workspace for writers who start with fragments: quick capture, a dedicated catalogue, simple backing and editable voice takes, then a useful mix or export when ready. This sharpens Daniel's stated direction without changing it into a production DAW. Preserving the current experience while improving trust is compatible with eventual paid distribution, but commercial readiness requires evidence beyond founder satisfaction.
